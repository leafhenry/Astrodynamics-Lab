# Earth texture

`earth_day.png` is the unmodified 2048 × 1024 equirectangular Blue Marble
texture downloaded from NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio.

- Source page: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/2915/
- Original file: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/vis/a000000/a002900/a002915/bluemarble-2048.png
- Credit: NASA/Goddard Space Flight Center Scientific Visualization Studio.
  Blue Marble data courtesy of Reto Stockli (NASA/GSFC) and NASA's Earth Observatory.
- NASA reproduction guidance: https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/
- Downloaded: 2026-11-15.
- SHA-256: `ae6214b078ed0864c96f74bcb10ae3021f6eb116f8059797efe0fa9ea8b89d35`.

The map is a satellite-observation composite, not live imagery. Its ocean color,
land elevation shading and ice coverage are baked into the original texture.
The rendering software adds solar lighting and Earth rotation. The starfield is
generated procedurally with a fixed random seed, not an observed star catalogue.
